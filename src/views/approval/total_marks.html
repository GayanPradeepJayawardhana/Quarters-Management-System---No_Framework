Railway Quarters Allotment - Evaluation & Marking Scheme

For Office Use Only : Marking Scheme
Description / Criteria Marks
1. Duration in the waiting list (1 mark per month)
2. Number of children (1 mark each for male children under 18 years and unmarried female children)
3. For residing in a rented house or an unlivable house
4. For essential service (Maximum 03 marks)
5. Permanency of service (12 marks deducted if temporary)
6. Distance to workplace (1 mark per kilometer starting from 15 km)
7. Serious illnesses of the applicant (02 marks)
8. Serious illnesses of dependents (01 mark)
9. Service duration in Transportation Sub-Department
10. Marital status (01 mark)
11. Total service duration (1 mark per year. If multiple officers have the same score, seniority is considered.)
